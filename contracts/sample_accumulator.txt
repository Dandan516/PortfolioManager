Underlying: Example Technology Inc. common stock (EXMPL)

The investor agrees to purchase 1,000 shares of EXMPL on each weekly fixing date at the accumulation price of USD 92.00 per share. If the official closing price is at or below the accumulation price, the investor will purchase 2,000 shares for that fixing date. If the closing price reaches or exceeds the knockout level of USD 108.00, the transaction terminates on that fixing date without further delivery obligations.
