SAMPLE CONTRACT - BARRIER NOTE

Issuer: Example Bank plc
Product: Barrier Note
Underlying: Example Semiconductor Index (ESI)
Currency: GBP
Trade Date: 2026-09-15
Maturity Date: 2028-09-15
Notional: GBP 25,000

The note has an initial index level of 4,000, a strike level of 4,000, and a knock-in barrier at 2,400. The barrier is monitored continuously during the term. If the barrier is never breached, the investor receives principal plus the positive performance of the index, subject to a maximum return of 18 percent.

If the barrier is breached, the maturity payment is determined by the final index performance and may be less than principal. Settlement is in cash. This document is a fictional sample for software testing only.
